PROJECT OVERVIEW

About DairySafe

A software prototype connecting milk screening with batch traceability and investigation support.

DS
FINAL-YEAR ENGINEERING PROJECT

DairySafe Intelligence Platform

Explainable AI-Based Milk Adulteration Detection and Risk-Aware Dairy Supply-Chain Traceability

Problem addressed

Milk quality checks, batch tracking and investigation records may be maintained separately. DairySafe demonstrates how these workflows can be linked within one software interface.

Project objectives

  • Enter six physicochemical milk parameters manually.
  • Demonstrate a transparent screening workflow.
  • Trace batches forward and backward through the supply chain.
  • Document risk investigation and laboratory verification.

Three modules

  1. Milk Adulteration Intelligence
  2. Dairy Batch Traceability
  3. Risk & Targeted Recall Support

Current limitations

  • No physical sensors or external backend.
  • LocalStorage is specific to this browser profile.
  • Screening rules are illustrative and not validated.
  • A trained model requires suitable labelled data and evaluation.
